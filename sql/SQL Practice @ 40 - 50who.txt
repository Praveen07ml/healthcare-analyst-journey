Display the names of customers who have purchased products from exactly 2 different categories.

Tables

Customers
Orders
Order_Items
Products

Business Requirement  -- it want customers purchased exactly 2 different categories
Manual Verification -- so we need to look each customer produtct category list if that customer purchased 2 different categories he was in our customer list
Business Proof -- group by + having + count distict categories = 2
SQL Pattern


SELECT c.customer_name
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o on o.customer_id = c.customer_id
JOIN order_items o1 on o1.order_id = o.order_id
JOIN products p on p.product_id = o1.product_id
GROUP by c.customer_id,c.customer_name
HAVING count(DISTINCT p.category_id) = 2;

Display the names of customers who have placed at least 5 orders, but whose latest order amount is less than ₹2,000.

Tables

Customers
Orders

Business Requirement -- we have to display the customer names who atleast purchased 5 orders and that their latest order must be less than 2k
Manual Verification -- we need to find that customers their total orders 5 or more and also and latest order amount less than 2k
Business Proof - count(order_count) >= 5 AND inside something max order date order amount less than 2k  its need inside of another

select c.customer_name
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o
on o.customer_id = c.customer_id
GROUP by c.customer_id,c.customer_name
HAVING count(o.order_id) >= 5 and (SELECT o2.total_amount 
                                   FROM orders o2
								   WHERE o2.customer_id = c.customer_id and o2.order_date = (SELECT max(o1.order_date)
								                                                           FROM orders o1
																						   WHERE o1.customer_id = c.customer_id  )) < 2000;

Business Requirement

Display the names of customers who have purchased every Electronics product.

Tables

Customers
Orders
Order_Items
Products



Business Requirement -- we need to display customers and they must purchase every product in electronics category
Manual Verification  -- we need to check in customer product category list that customer purchase every product in electronics category
Business Proof  -- group by + having + count of distinct products = count of every product in electronics 
SQL Pattern



SELECT c.customer_name
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o on o.customer_id = c.customer_id
JOIN order_items od on od.order_id = o.order_id
JOIN products p on p.product_id = od.product_id
JOIN categories cat on cat.category_id = p.category_id
WHERE cat.category_name = 'Electronics'
GROUP by c.customer_id,c.customer_name
HAVING count(DISTINCT p.product_id) = (SELECT count(p.product_id)
                                       FROM products p
									   JOIN categories c1
									   on c1.category_id = p.category_id
									   WHERE c1.category_name = 'Electronics');

Display the names of customers whose total spending is greater than ₹50,000 and who have purchased at least 10 different products.

Tables

Customers
Orders
Order_Items





Business Requirement --- we have display customers total spend is greater than 50 k and purchased 10 different products
Manual Verification -- in our customr total spending must be 50 k and and also purchase 10 different products
Business Proof -- group by + having + sum(total_spend) > 50 k and count of distincts products more than or equal to 10 
SQL Pattern

SELECT c.customer_name
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o on o.customer_id = c.customer_id

Display the names of customers who have placed more than 3 orders, and every order amount is greater than ₹2,000.

Tables

Customers
Orders

(Hint: Think about how to prove "every order.")

Business Requirement -- displaying the customers their orders count more than 3 orders and also we need to make sure their each order grater than 2000
Manual Verification -- so we need to check the whole orders more than three and each order value must be more than 2000 for each customer
Business Proof -- group by + having + count of orders >= 3 and not exist (violation)
SQL Pattern

SELECT c.customer_name
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o 
on o.customer_id = c.customer_id
where not EXISTS (SELECT 1
                  FROM orders o
				  where o.customer_id = c.customer_id and o.total_amount < 2000)
GROUP by c.customer_id,c.customer_name
HAVING count(o.order_id) > 3;







Display the names of customers who have purchased from every category except Electronics.

Tables

Customers
Orders
Order_Items
Products
Categories



Business Requirement -- we need to display customers they must purchase all categories  except Electronics
Manual Verification -- we need to check the customer must purchase all categories except one electronics category
Business Proof -- count of distinct customer purchased categories = count of actual categories + group by + having
SQL Pattern

SELECT c.customer_name
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o on o.customer_id = c.customer_id
JOIN order_items o1 on o1.order_id = o.order_id
JOIN products p on p.product_id = o1.product_id
JOIN categories cat on cat.category_id = p.category_id
WHERE cat.category_name <> 'Electronics'
GROUP by c.customer_id,c.customer_name
HAVING count(DISTINCT p.category_id) = (SELECT count(p1.product_id)
                                        FROM products p1
										JOIN categories c1
										on c1.category_id = p1.category_id
										WHERE c1.category_name <> 'Electronics');
