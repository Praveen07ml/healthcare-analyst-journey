Display the names of customers who have placed exactly one order.

Tables Available

Customers
Orders

Business Requirement - it wants customer names who ordered only one order
Manual Verification - so we need to find customers who placed only one order which mean in customer order list if a customer placed only one order we have to select it right
Business Proof - group by + having + count(o.order_id)
SQL Pattern 

SELECT c.customer_name
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o
on o.customer_id = c.customer_id
GROUP by c.customer_id,c.customer_name
HAVING count(o.order_id) = 1;

Display the names of customers who have purchased every Footwear product.

Tables Available

Orders
Order_Items
Products
Categories



Business Requirement  -- we have to find the customers each product in footwear section must by that customer
Manual Verification -- in this foot wear section products all products buy by that customer is in our list 
Business Proof - group by + having + count( distinct products) = actual count of footwear products
SQL Pattern

-- Display the names of customers who have purchased every Footwear product.

SELECT c.customer_name,p.product_name
FROM customers c
JOIN orders o on o.customer_id = c.customer_id
JOIN order_items o1 on o1.order_id = o.order_id
JOIN products p on p.product_id = o1.product_id
JOIN categories cat on cat.category_id = p.category_id
GROUP by  c.customer_id, c.customer_name,p.product_name
HAVING count(DISTINCT o1.product_id) = (SELECT count(p.product_id)
                                        FROM products p 
										JOIN categories c on c.category_id = p.category_id
										WHERE c.category_name = 'Footwear');

Categories



Business Requirement  - we have to find customers atleast purchase every category product and their total quantity is greater than 20
Manual Verification -- in customer order purchase that cover whole categories at least one customer and their total quantity must be greater than 20 times 

Business Proof - in exists we have to apply conditions every product + sum(quantity ) > 20
SQL Pattern

SELECT c.customer_name
FROM customers c
WHERE EXISTS (SELECT 1
              FROM orders o
			  JOIN order_items o1 on o1.order_id = o.order_id
			  JOIN products p on p.product_id = o1.product_id
			  WHERE o.customer_id = c.customer_id
			  GROUP by o.customer_id
			  HAVING (count(DISTINCT p.category_id) = (SELECT count(category_id)
			                                          FROM categories)) and sum(o1.quantity) > 20);
